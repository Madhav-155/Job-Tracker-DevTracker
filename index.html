FAQ

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB and localStorage. No data leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

How do I backup my data?

Go to Settings > Data Management and click "Export All Data (JSON)". This will download a complete backup.

Can I use this offline?

Yes! After the initial load, the app works completely offline.

How do I add a new field to job entries?

See the README.md file for developer instructions on extending the DATA_MODEL.
